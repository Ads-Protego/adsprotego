# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

AdsProtego takes security seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting AdsProtego, please do not report it publicly through GitHub Issues.

Instead, contact our security team at:

**security@adsprotego.com**

If you are unable to use that address, contact:

**office@adsprotego.com**

Please include as much detail as possible, including:

- A description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability
- The affected component or service
- Any relevant screenshots or logs
- Potential impact
- Your contact information, if you would like a response

## Responsible Disclosure

We ask security researchers to:

- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that does not belong to them
- Avoid disrupting AdsProtego services
- Avoid exposing vulnerabilities publicly before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and respond
- Act in good faith and within applicable laws

## Response

We aim to review legitimate security reports as quickly as possible.

Where appropriate, we may contact the reporter for additional information and provide updates during the investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies to the AdsProtego platform and official services operated by YVE MEDIA ADS SRL.

For general product support, please contact:

**support@adsprotego.com**

Official website:

https://adsprotego.com
