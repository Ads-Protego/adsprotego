# AdsProtego Integrations

AdsProtego is designed to work with major advertising platforms and infrastructure services used by businesses and agencies.

## Advertising platforms

AdsProtego supports protection and monitoring workflows for:

- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- TikTok Ads
- Microsoft Ads

## Google Ads

AdsProtego can connect with Google Ads to help advertisers monitor campaign activity, analyze suspicious traffic and manage protection workflows.

## Meta Ads

AdsProtego supports monitoring and protection workflows for Meta Ads campaigns.

## TikTok Ads

AdsProtego supports monitoring and protection workflows for TikTok Ads campaigns.

## Microsoft Ads

AdsProtego supports monitoring and protection workflows for Microsoft Ads campaigns.

## Cloudflare

Cloudflare integration can provide an additional layer of traffic control and blocking capabilities.

## Additional integrations

AdsProtego continues to expand its integration ecosystem as the platform evolves.
