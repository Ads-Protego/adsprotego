# AdsProtego

**Ad fraud protection and traffic monitoring for Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Microsoft Ads.**

AdsProtego is a SaaS platform that helps businesses and agencies detect suspicious advertising activity, monitor paid traffic and reduce wasted advertising spend across major advertising platforms.

## Why AdsProtego

Paid advertising campaigns can be affected by repeated clicks, suspicious traffic, bots and other patterns that reduce campaign efficiency.

AdsProtego provides an additional layer of visibility and protection by helping advertisers monitor traffic behavior, identify suspicious activity and react faster when unusual patterns appear.

## What AdsProtego does

- Detects suspicious and repeated clicks
- Monitors IP activity
- Helps identify potentially fraudulent traffic
- Supports automated and manual IP blocking
- Provides campaign-level monitoring
- Detects unusual traffic patterns
- Provides alerts and reporting
- Helps analyze suspicious behavior
- Gives advertisers better visibility into paid traffic

## How it works

AdsProtego monitors advertising traffic and analyzes activity associated with paid campaigns.

When suspicious or repeated behavior is detected, the platform helps advertisers investigate the activity and apply protection rules based on their campaign settings.

Depending on the integration and configuration, AdsProtego can assist with traffic monitoring, IP-based controls and automated protection workflows.
